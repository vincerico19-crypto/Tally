Cards & accounts

Pay a card

Paying a card down? Log it here and both sides move at once — the card drops, the account it came from drops, and your spending total is untouched.

Balances, limits & due dates

Enter what the account said on a given day. Everything you have logged since that day is added or subtracted automatically — so setting the date back to the start of the month lets your transactions do the maths.

Backup

Build

Everything lives in this browser only. Save a backup before clearing browser data or switching phones — restoring replaces what is stored now. On a phone this opens the share sheet, so you can pick a folder, drop it in iCloud Drive, or send it to yourself.

Paste anything

Copy straight from your bank app, a statement, a receipt, or your notes and drop it in. For a screenshot, press and hold the text in Photos, choose Select All, then Copy — then hit the button below. Tally reads the dates and amounts itself and shows you what it found before anything saves.

No format needed. A line just needs a number somewhere in it. A missing date falls back to the date above it, then to today. Running balances, tax lines, and reference numbers get ignored. Refunds, deposits, and payouts are read as money in.
